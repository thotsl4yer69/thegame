# Credits and provenance

## Original game artwork

The protagonist, eight-woman baddie roster, male enemy/boss roster, pigeon, props, pickups, effects and four venue backdrops were generated specifically for **THOTSL4YER69 — Just The Tip**, then chroma-keyed, cleaned, normalised, resized and integrated as production sprites for this project. No SmutBase, Itch.io or OpenGameArt files are redistributed in the release.

SmutBase/Open3DLab, adult asset listings on Itch.io and OpenGameArt were used only as market/style research. The decision to create one original set avoids uncertain uploader-specific rights and inconsistent “asset salad” art direction.
