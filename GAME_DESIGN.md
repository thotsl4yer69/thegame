# Final game design

## Core loop

Enter venue → clear two mixed-archetype waves → defeat boss → choose a corrupting upgrade → recover 30 meat → enter the next venue. The player balances immediate aggression against the HIGH meter: successful combos create HIGH, taking damage destroys it, packets force it to 100, and pigeon support consumes it.

## Player expression

The quick combo controls crowds, heavy attacks create breathing room, dash passes through danger, weapons reward scavenging, and packets create short high-damage windows. At full HIGH, heavy attack becomes the MONEY SHOT finisher. Cash is also tactical: MAKE IT RAIN costs $50 but distracts nearby female baddies. Upgrade combinations define each run: healing aggression, repeated pigeon clears, heavy-hit builds, packet builds or cash/score greed.

## Tone

The target is an underground Y2K arcade cabinet produced after an irresponsible night out: erotic nightclub fashion, ugly male grotesques, vulgar dialogue, hot-pink/cyan rim lighting, wet floors, bad drugs, worse economics and a pigeon that solves problems from the air.

## Completion state

The game has a character-driven title/menu, three difficulty profiles, an unlockable after-dark gallery, four finite stages, twelve combat waves, four bosses with unique specials, four venue hazards, three intermissions, eight achievements, persistent score, game-over/retry and a final dawn ending. It is a complete compact browser game rather than an endless prototype loop.
